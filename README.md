# ESP32 Remote Controlled Car

## Description: An ESP32 remote controlled car involving assembly, electronics, troubleshooting and programming

## Overview:
I built and programmed a remote-controlled robot car using an ESP32 microcontroller, integrating Infrared sensors with an Osoyoo kit. I gained experience combining mechanical assembly, electronics and programming to create a functioning car.

## Tasks I completed:

- Assembled the robot chassis along with the mechanical components
- Integrated the electronic components such as the motor, and ESP32 as well as the wheels
- Programmed the movements of the robot and the functions of the remote control
- Troubleshot any issues that arose within the code
- Made minor adjustments to ensure the optimisation of the car

## Mechanical Assembly
This project gave me a strong foundation in the ways in which mechanical components sit alongside electrical components. It taught me how to integrate each of the parts into the build without compromising the appearance or the function.

## Electronics & Programming
As a mechanical engineering student, I wanted to gain a better understanding of electrical components and how they work alongside mechanical components. I worked with:

- ESP32
- DC motors
- Motor driver
- Battery/ Power supply
- C++

## What I learned:
Mainly I learnt troubleshooting and how to test each part of the system without having to go back to the beginning. I did this by checking the power supply, wiring, connections, motors and checking the code. I gained a lot of experience in isolating individual components rather than beginning the project again.\
\
This project also made me interested in developing my ability to work with mechanical aspects of engineering alongside the electronic/electrical aspects.

## Areas for improvement:
I am hoping to revisit this project soon to make the car more autonomous in two stages:/
1. **Avoiding obstacles** I am planning to add a HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor to detect obstacles
2. **Following a line** After this I will add a TCRT5000 infrared reflectance sensor. This would allow my car to follow a black line on the floor while avoiding obstacles
